Frequently Asked Questions

Can I view a bike before buying?

Yes. Viewings are by appointment in Swindon or Marlborough.

Do you offer delivery?

Local delivery within approximately 30 miles can be arranged. UK delivery may also be available depending on the bicycle.

Can I reserve a bike?

Send a WhatsApp message or use Book a Viewing. A reservation is confirmed only after TomyBike replies.

What if the bike I need is not listed?

Use the Looking for a Bike form. TomyBike can check suppliers for a suitable model, size and budget.
